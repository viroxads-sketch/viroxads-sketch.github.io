Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

1. Introduction

At Virox Games ("we", "our", or "us"), we respect the privacy of our players. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile games (including Bol Jump, Lex Ball) and visit our website.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to ensure the proper functioning and improvement of our games:

3. Children's Privacy

Our games are designed to be safe and enjoyable for a broad audience. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
