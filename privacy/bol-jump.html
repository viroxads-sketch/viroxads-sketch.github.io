Privacy Policy for Bol Jump

Last updated: October 11, 2026

1. Introduction

At Virox Games ("we", "our", or "us"), we respect the privacy of our players. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you play our mobile game Bol Jump.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to ensure proper game functionality, save progress, and maintain leaderboards:

  • Google Play Games Services: If you choose to sign in, we collect your Google Play Games ID and Display Name for authentication, profile management, and leaderboard tracking.
  • Non-Personal Data: Device type, operating system version, and anonymous analytics regarding gameplay and crash reports to fix bugs.
  • Advertising Identifiers: In accordance with Google Play policies, third-party advertising partners (such as AdMob) may use device identifiers to serve relevant ads.

3. Children's Privacy

Our game is designed to be safe and enjoyable for a broad audience. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access or loss. Data transmitted via Google Play Services and Firebase is encrypted.
